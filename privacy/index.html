Privacy Policy

VHDL Manual is designed with a Data Not Collected privacy posture.

Effective date: February 20, 2026

Summary

  • We do not collect personal data from this app.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, ad SDKs, or tracking technologies in the app.
  • We do not sell data and do not share data with data brokers.

Data stored on your device

VHDL Manual stores app settings and reading state locally on your device to support app features such as bookmarks, recent history, and interface preferences. This data stays on-device and is not transmitted to us.

Tracking and third parties

VHDL Manual does not track you across apps or websites and does not use third-party partners for cross-app tracking.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date above before those changes take effect.
