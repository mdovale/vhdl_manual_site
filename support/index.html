Support

Need help with VHDL Manual? Contact us and include your iOS version and a short issue description.

Email: vhdlmanual.bobble124@passmail.com

Manual content does not appear

  1. Force close and reopen VHDL Manual.
  2. Restart your iPhone, then open the app again.
  3. Verify you are using the latest App Store version.

Widget does not refresh

  1. Long-press the widget and choose Edit Widget.
  2. Remove and re-add the VHDL of the Day widget.
  3. Open the app once to refresh the daily section state.

Search results seem incomplete

  1. Try a shorter search term or alternate keyword.
  2. Clear active filters and search again.
  3. Confirm content opens correctly from Browse view.

When contacting support

Please include:

  • App version number
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • Screenshots if available
